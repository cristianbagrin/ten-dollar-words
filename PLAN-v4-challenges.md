# Writing challenges: the full list, for approval one by one

**How a challenge works (common to all of them):** a **Challenge** button (in the Lab for now) has three choices: **Roll** for one random challenge, **Roll a combo** for 2 or 3 that fit together, or **Pick** one from the list. The active challenge sits in a small card with a live checklist (✓ / ✗ per rule). When every rule holds, the card stamps **Challenge met**, in the same style as the "Over budget" stamp, with the coin sound. Challenges never block typing. They only check (rule 1 of the plan: the tool marks, it doesn't stop you). They're saved per draft, and the account carries them. Ghosted text and Overflow don't count.

Legend for **How**:
- **Local:** checked live in the browser, no AI key needed.
- **AI sets:** the AI reads your draft or topic once to make up the challenge, then the app checks it locally.
- **AI judges:** the AI decides at the end whether you met it (a "Judge it" button). Used only where a rule can't be counted.

| B3 | **Five-word rule** | Include a 1-word sentence, a short one (2 to 5 words) and a long one (over 25 words). | Local |

| B7 | **Two-line paragraphs** | No paragraph over 2 sentences. | Local |
| B8 | **Hook in 8** | The first sentence is 8 words or fewer. | Local |

## E. Adapted to what you're writing (AI)

The AI reads the draft so far, or a one-line "what's this about" if the draft is empty.

| # | Challenge | What you do | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Ban the obvious** | The AI names the 5 words everyone uses for this topic, and you can't use them. | AI sets |
| E2 | **Jargon jail** | The AI lists your field's jargon in this draft, and you rewrite without it. | AI sets |
| E3 | **Surprise words** | 3 unexpected but usable words picked for your topic. Work them in. | AI sets |
| E4 | **Borrowed field** | Explain your point with an analogy from a field the AI rolls (cooking, sailing, chess…). | AI sets, AI judges |
| E5 | **Write to the ending** | The AI gives you a last line, and you write toward it. | AI sets |
| E6 | **Start in the middle** | The AI picks a vivid moment from your topic, and you open there with no setup. | AI sets |
| E7 | **Argue the other side first** | Open with the best case against your point, then win anyway. | AI sets (the counterpoint), AI judges |
| E8 | **Explain it to a 10-year-old** | Grade 3 or under, and the AI checks nothing important got lost. | Local + AI judges |
| E9 | **Skeptic in the room** | After each paragraph the AI asks a skeptical reader's question in the margin, and your next paragraph answers it. | AI sets |
| E10 | **Show, don't claim** | The AI marks each abstract claim, and you back every one with a concrete example. | AI sets, AI judges |
| E11 | **The story** | Include one personal story from your own life that carries the point. | AI judges |
| E12 | **Contrarian title** | The AI suggests a counterintuitive angle on your topic, and you write the piece that earns it. | AI sets |
| E13 | **Genre swap** | Write it as a recipe, a weather forecast, a court verdict, a sports commentary or a field guide entry (rolled). | AI sets the genre, AI judges |
| E14 | **Five hooks** | Write 5 openings as alternatives on your first line. The AI picks the strongest, blind, and says why. | Uses alternatives, AI judges |
| E15 | **Letter to one person** | The AI suggests a specific reader for this topic ("a nervous first-time founder"). Write only to them. | AI sets |

## F. Combos and difficulty

| # | Challenge | What you do | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Roll a combo** | Stacks 2 or 3 challenges that fit together. Clashes are never paired (Drabble + Six-word story, Grade 5 + Ten-dollar word…). | Local |
| F2 | **Difficulty dial** | Easy / Medium / Hard tunes the numbers: Short fuse at 15, 12 or 8 words; Lipogram on z, then t, then e; Sprint at 100, 150 or 250 words. | Local |
| F3 | **Streak** | A quiet count of days in a row with a challenge met, shown only in the Challenge menu. | Local |
